# Planethunt evidence and publication snapshot — 13 October 2026

A dated public record of the archival analysis for **KIC 5689121 / TIC 120686465**, plus an RNAAS manuscript draft with **Kuber Mehta as the sole listed author**.

The 13.769-day signal remains an **unconfirmed candidate**. The exploratory modelled false-positive probability is 6.9%; an unresolved companion remains a possible host. Archival follow-up has not produced an independent transit recovery or a measured close-companion contrast curve. Prior-search identity and novelty remain provisional.

## Preserved in this release

- Kepler photometry, transit-model products, reserved-quarter tests, dilution and centroid comparisons, and false-positive sensitivity calculations.
- The expanded archive audit, including Spitzer, TESS/TGLC, ZTF/PTF/WISE, CFHT and joint target/neighbour SPHEREx products, with query provenance and limitations.
- A 1,074-word RNAAS draft, 124-word abstract, one code-rendered two-panel figure, editable AASTeX source, plain text, review PDF, source hashes and claim-to-evidence pointers.
- A proposed cover note and submission checklist. The manuscript is **not submitted or accepted** and still needs the author's review and affiliation/email.
- Citation metadata, repository inventory and checksums. No new blanket licence is granted; existing third-party terms remain in force.
