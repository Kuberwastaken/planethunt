The short duration needs explanation.

A grazing geometry was suggested in an earlier fit, but its saved fit output is absent. We do not promote that suggestion into a measured impact parameter or radius. No formal FPP, completed independent holdout, or complete negative-control results are included.

Counts, odd/even test and time conventions

The cache has 17 sector labels and 92,860 cadences. The search counts 46 events; pixel localization uses 45 events in 16 sectors. These are different subsets. Odd/even depths differ by 1.33 sigma. That alone does not rule out an eclipsing binary. The saved depth_half_ppm is not a phase-0.5 secondary eclipse.

Verify original FITS time conventions before scheduling any follow-up from the numeric epoch. The digest does not provide an observing schedule.

How much was searched?

Target selection, assigned work, inherited results and completed searches are different things. The bars below use a shared 0-600-star scale; they are not a success-rate chart.

TESS extension: 540 unique assigned targets
54 new local outputs counted in the reconciliation. Remote completion counts are reported; full per-star evidence is not shipped. First-600 slice membership is approximate.
Inherited upstream TESS results: 526
Not newly searched by this run. Not combined with assigned counts to advertise a larger completed survey.
Codex TESS pilot: 175 stars
350 two-band runs. 44 stars affected by QLP timing errors; 88 repeats removed those products. Revised holdouts are retrospective.
Codex Kepler Bonus pilot: 64 stars
Selected from 1,800 eligible background M dwarfs. Other named archives were leads, not completed scans.
Control tests are diagnostics, not planet probabilities.

Codex reports five initial screen passes in 100 inverted TESS searches, and zero in 64 inverted Kepler searches. Known-planet recoveries are controls, not discoveries. The TESS extension's stored injection log is incomplete and does not establish recovery completeness.

QLP's barycentric timing issue for sectors 99-104 is independently documented by MAST. Source-reported run counts are not independently reproduced here.

Reproduction and large files

Original TESS scripts retain historical scratch paths and depend on an incomplete upstream environment. Codex's bootstrap needs requirement and measurement files not yet received. Neither pack is one-command reproducible from this partial release.

Large data should be checksummed release assets or source-download manifests rather than oversized git blobs. The present tree is small and includes the two TESS NPZ caches. Additional raw archives will be audited before addition.
