NEXT SCIENTIFIC USE

and neighbour WCS positions and edge coverage, then fit their infrared fluxes

here. Roughly 1.8–2 arcsec resolution can help separate the 7.1-arcsec neighbour,
but cannot replace an AO contrast curve for a much closer unresolved companion.
