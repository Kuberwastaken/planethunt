Hi Steve,

Thank you — that sent me down a useful path. I checked the Exoplanet Archive and followed through to the linked archives. I haven’t found usable high-resolution imaging of this target. A public CFHT image resolves the known 7″ pair, but both cores are saturated, so I can’t use it to constrain a close companion reliably.

I also reduced archival Spitzer exposures, extracted joint SPHEREx spectra for the known pair, and checked TESS, TGLC, ZTF, PTF and WISE photometry. None provides an independent transit detection: Spitzer and SPHEREx miss the predicted transits, and the other measurements are too noisy or sparsely sampled for a useful test at this depth. The unresolved-host ambiguity remains.

One unexpected lead: the exact Gaia source appears in Barber et al. (2022), THYME VIII, with a Kepler-data flag. I plan to ask the authors which light curve they searched and whether they examined this period; I’m not assuming membership in the young association.

The searches, reductions and limitations are here:
https://github.com/Kuberwastaken/planethunt/tree/main/analysis/archive-exhaustion-20261012
