# Public website rebuild — 12 October 2026

This is a presentation change. Scientific status is still the 11 October validation snapshot.
No original measurements, source archives, or evidence-pack checksums were changed.

The site now leads with the candidate and a real Pan-STARRS image, then progresses through
observations, sky context, vetting, conditional false-positive modelling, physical inferences,
other search outcomes, follow-up, and open evidence. Detailed diagnostics and original dated
sections remain available in expandable sections.

## Data and science handling

- The dynamic transit plot reads saved 15-minute bins and a sampled smooth transit model.
- The full light-curve view includes all 46,523 saved detrended cadences; it discloses points
  outside its plotted vertical range and links the complete CSV. Quarter filters use recorded
  quarter values only.
- The event view explicitly uses the 74-event / 23 reserved-quarter export, distinct from the
  strict original 70 / 22 selection. A full-baseline display is not a new blind holdout.
- FPP is described as conditional modelling. Hypothetical imaging remains visibly hypothetical;
  the baseline scenario table remains labelled as baseline when changing the what-if control.
- The radius, orbit, insolation and equilibrium temperature comparisons are conditional on the
  assumed host. Reflectivity changes a simple equilibrium model, not measured weather.
- The estimated light-emission epoch is around 1040 CE using the actual 2009–2013 observations
  and the approximate 298 pc distance, rather than subtracting travel time from today's year.
- The 1,736 null searches are source searches (1,648 unique KICs), not all independent unique stars.
- Season 3's two-event pixel result is qualified rather than being presented as strong localization.

## Images and charts

Twenty SVG diagnostics are built from saved outputs, plus code-native scale comparisons.
The added Lyra locator uses the approximate coordinates in the original `post_where.py`.
Pan-STARRS and DSS2 raw cutouts are reused without changing their pixels. Clean 2MASS and
AllWISE renders were fetched from CDS hips2fits at the existing centre and field size, with
parameters and attribution recorded in `docs/sky/SOURCES.json`. Catalogue-position markers,
scale bars and north/east labels are rendered separately as SVG. The original registered
image panels are retained for inspection. Coarse surveys do not resolve every marked source.

## Second design revision: evidence atlas

Replaced the story-led composition with an industrial, black-and-white observation record.
Barlow Condensed Light sets the oversized target identifier; IBM Plex Mono sets compact
measurement labels and body text. Fonts are self-hosted with their OFL notices.

The measured transit and actual target image now lead the page. A six-row evidence map links
questions, numerical tests and their saved outputs. Source-localization plots are visible in
the main reading flow, followed by a dilution-model table, physical equations and a parameter
ledger with asymmetric errors. Monochrome survey presentation can be switched to original colour.

Five additional diagnostics are generated by `evidence_atlas.py`: quarter coverage, folded
residuals, all-event depth distribution, source/ephemeris comparison and centroid predictions.
Residuals subtract a linearly interpolated saved median model; this is not a refit. The histogram
includes all 74 exported events (23 reserved), including the >8,000 ppm event. Quarter counts
sum to all 46,523 samples. All plotted residual error bars fit the chosen axis range.
The existing scenario chart now groups TP, PTP and DTP under planet-on-target, consistent with
TRICERATOPS definitions and the accompanying table. Original scenario probabilities are unchanged.

The revised layout was checked at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 CSS pixels with no document overflow.
Event and reserved-event views, full light curve, original survey colour, chart enlargement and
console state were rechecked. Chart text and numerical attributes were inspected for clipping
and invalid values. Full data and local-link validation was rerun after the redesign.

## Planet gallery, share assets and source-chart spacing

Added four 4:3 generated planet views: exterior, atmospheric limb, hypothetical cutaway and
star/planet perspective. Every scene is labelled as an interpretation; exact structure and
composition remain unknown. A matched wide share card, code-native SVG favicon, PNG sizes
and ICO fallback are included. Full-size gallery WebPs total 231,248 bytes; PNG originals and
all prompts remain in the repository. Responsive sources use native 4:3 dimensions.

The existing physical-model section now pairs images with derived comparisons and keeps
equations, fitted parameters and code-rendered scale diagrams accessible. Added values are
computed from saved inputs, with circular-orbit and spherical-volume assumptions explicit.
The earlier rounded post values are represented by the saved 417 K equilibrium model and
approximately 1040 CE light-emission epoch. Mass and gravity are not inferred.

Rebuilt the crowded centroid figure as a full-width source ranking with dedicated source,
logarithmic mark and value columns. Values remain unchanged. The chart scrolls within its
container on small screens and retains the enlarged view.
