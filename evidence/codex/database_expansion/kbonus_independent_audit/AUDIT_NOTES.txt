Original pilot outputs remain unchanged and must not be quoted as corrected SAP significance.

Independent shape check
- Protected local linear or quadratic baselines use only points outside twice the BLS full duration; no transit clipping and no global detrending are used for the shape plots.
- Period remains 13.769413569662944 days. Physical shape parameters fit discovery quarters only; holdout shape is frozen except an independent depth scale and constant baseline.
- The signal is visibly U-shaped in both PSF and aperture photometry. Held-out amplitude is about 80% of the discovery template.
- With catalog-agent Gaia values M=0.5446 and R=0.5553 solar, a/R=35.55 and circular central maximum duration about3.10h. Fixed-density fits give b=0.66-0.76, contact duration2.14-2.42h, radius ratio0.04885-0.05099. Approximate undiluted target-hosted radius would be2.96-3.09Earth radii. Stellar errors, eccentricity, dilution, unresolved companions, and limb-darkening uncertainties prevent treating this as a measurement with validated error bars.
- Free-density fits are degenerate with limb darkening/impact parameter at this cadence. Do not present them as a precise density measurement.
- SAP depths depend strongly on spacecraft orientation, while PSF depths are more consistent; this motivates pixel localization and crowding assessment.

Pixel localization audit
- Root difference images use original primary-target Kepler TPF pixels with official calibrated Kepler PRFs. Correct row/column order and +0.5 convention verified against installed Lightkurve source and original FITS physical WCS headers.
- Root two-source quiescent-image common astrometric offset is small. Independent fits to saved difference images without that offset retain candidate preference in all13quarters, sum delta chi2=797.72.
- Replacing a constant background with an independent spatial plane retains candidate preference in all13quarters, sum delta chi2=579.27 with no offset and581.06 with offset. Every fitted target amplitude is positive.
- This supports localization near the candidate relative to the bright star7.09arcsec away. It does not rule out an unresolved background/companion eclipsing binary at the candidate position. Shared root difference images mean this is a model stress test, not a second independent extraction.
- PRF mismatch, neighboring unmodeled sources, and interpixel covariances remain; delta chi2 values are descriptive only, not calibrated FPP or discovery odds.
